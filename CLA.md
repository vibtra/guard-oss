# Vibtra Guard Individual Contributor Licence Agreement

> **Draft, not lawyer-reviewed.** Version 0.1, 28 October 2026. Both founders approve this text on the pull request before it applies to anyone. A lawyer reviews it before the company is incorporated.

This agreement is modelled on the [Apache Software Foundation Individual Contributor License Agreement](https://www.apache.org/licenses/icla.pdf) (v2.2) and the [Harmony](https://www.harmonyagreements.org/) contributor agreements, with the outbound licensing choice Harmony calls "any licence".

Thank you for contributing to Vibtra Guard (the "Project"). This agreement says what rights you give the Copyright Holders in your Contributions. You keep the copyright in your Contributions. Read it before you sign.

- **"You"** means the individual who signs this agreement.
- **"Copyright Holders"** means Sam Rahmani and Tyler Stewart until the company that will own the Project is incorporated. From then on it means that company, which receives their rights by assignment, and its successors and assigns. *(Pending: the company's legal name is added here when it exists.)*
- **"Contribution"** means any original work of authorship, including changes or additions to an existing work, that You intentionally submit to the Copyright Holders for inclusion in the Project. "Submit" means any form of electronic, verbal or written communication sent to the Copyright Holders or their representatives, including pull requests, issues, patches and comments in the Project's repositories. A communication that You clearly mark in writing as "Not a Contribution" is not a Contribution.

The licence in section 2 includes the right to distribute Your Contributions under any licence, including the open-source licences the Project uses today (AGPL-3.0-only and Apache-2.0) and commercial licences such as the Vibtra Enterprise Licence and the terms of a hosted service. You do not have to support Your Contributions. The Copyright Holders do not have to use them.

## 7. Work that is not Yours

If You submit work that is not Your original creation, You say so in the pull request, name its source and its licence, and include any notices it carries. The Project's `REUSE.toml` records such files.

## How to sign

Add this line to the description of your first pull request, with your name and the email address on your commits:

```
I agree to the Vibtra Guard Individual Contributor Licence Agreement, version 0.1 (CLA.md).
Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>
```

A maintainer does not merge a pull request from a new contributor until that line is present. The agreement then covers all your later Contributions to the Project. If the agreement changes, you sign the new version only for Contributions made after it.
